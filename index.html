Strengths and weaknesses

  • Weak: small businesses with their own registered IP blocks (SWIP) on Charter/Comcast. Where Kickfire and IPinfo share a domain, Kickfire is right on 37 IPs and Leadfeeder on only 10.
  • Strong: IPs routed through an MSP or an education/government network. Leadfeeder returns the organization actually using the IP: OARnet becomes University of Akron, State of NC becomes Nash County.

Conditions to replace Kickfire

  • Rewrite checkValid and map company_type to flags (Leadfeeder has no isISP/isMobile/isWifi).
  • Normalize domains from url and add a junk-domain blacklist.
  • Source NAICS/SIC entirely from CAPI.
  • Use the manual labeling results to decide who is right when domains differ.
  • Get an API-only plan quote and review the DPA.

Agree group: 300 mismatched IPs (30%)

Kickfire and IPinfo gave the same domain; Leadfeeder returned something different.

In the “entirely different company” bucket (114 IPs): some are renames or mergers that name matching misses (AES Indiana, formerly Indianapolis Power & Light; Cellphone-Mate and SureCall). Some may be cases where Leadfeeder is more correct: Kickfire and IPinfo both rely on WHOIS, so they return whoever registered the IP block (an MSP or IT provider), while Leadfeeder relies on traffic and returns the organization actually using the IP (Network Outsource / Central Islip Schools, State of NC / Nash County). Some are clearly wrong, such as sourcing.hktdc.com for American Dehydrated Foods.

Trial quota: 3,468 of 5,000 used, about 1,500 matches left.

  • 4,452 IPs were called but only 3,468 matches were consumed. The other 984 were network providers or had no company data.
  • 5,000 is the trial’s match limit, confirmed by Leadfeeder via live chat.
  • The ~1,500 remaining matches are for further testing. This figure does not yet subtract a few manual calls made in Postman.
  • Trial expiry does not auto-bill: Leadfeeder confirmed that when the trial ends or the 5,000 matches run out, the account does not switch to a paid plan and no invoice is generated. Past the limit, the API only returns a quota_exceeded error.

Throughput: 2 threads, ~2.1 requests/second. About 10 transient network errors, all retried successfully.

Prepared: 2026-10-22 · Purpose: choose an IP-to-company service to replace Kickfire in the Handle_RevealedCompanies flow

5. Pros and cons

Leadfeeder

Pros

  • Verified on real data. Coverage on par with Kickfire, and it identifies the real organization behind an MSP or an education network (e.g. OARnet → University of Akron).
  • confidence is usable: dropping the low level removes most of the junk from hosting/VPN/mobile.
  • Many fields. Includes parent company, which supports merging subsidiaries later.
  • The terms explicitly allow using the data in our own product (“Integrated Data Offering”).
  • Trial already used, and the working process with sales is clear.

Cons

  • Data must be deleted within 30 days of contract termination. The contract auto-renews for 12 months.
  • Domains need normalization. 11% of results have no domain. Revenue comes back in multiple currencies.
  • Weak on small businesses with their own registered IP blocks (SWIP).
  • shared_network also filters out universities and hospitals. Customers may lose these companies compared with Kickfire.

Snitcher

Pros

  • The T&C do not require deleting data on termination.
  • Clean domain. Has a phone number. type classifies business/edu/gov the way our system already does.
  • The rate limit is clearly published (600 requests/minute).
  • EU legal entity (Netherlands), Dutch law applies.

Cons

  • Quality is unverified. The public benchmark is low (precision 20%).
  • No real confidence score, no lat/long, and LinkedIn is the only social network.
  • The 202 status makes the Lambda flow more complex.
  • The public T&C are generic SaaS terms, with no API-specific terms.
  • No self-service trial. Trial credits may be very few.

8. How to test Snitcher

  1. Get a key: fill in the form at https://www.snitcher.com/ip-to-company-api or contact sales. Ask for a trial key with about 5,000 credits, plus the questions in section 7.
  2. Quick try in Postman:
    • POST https://api.snitcher.com/company/find?ip={ip}, header Authorization: Bearer {key}.
    • Check remaining credits: https://api.snitcher.com/company/usage.
  3. Benchmark:
    • Run 200 IPs first and compare against /company/usage to learn whether 404s consume credits.
    • Then run all 4,452 IPs already tested on Leadfeeder.
    • If credits run short, run the 300 hand-labeled IPs.
    • Compare coverage, network-provider detection rate, field coverage, and accuracy on the labeled set.
  4. Script: reuse research_notes/Dịch vụ thay thế Kickfire/leadfeeder/lf_bench/run.js (caches raw responses, has backoff) and add handling for the 202 status.
